PRINCIPLES

Principles

What all four have in common.

01

One job, nothing more

Deciding what not to build comes first. Each product opens straight to what it does, with only the features that job needs.

02

Only the data the job needs

Collection is limited to what is needed to provide and improve each feature. Shikko Watch keeps asset data on the device, and gamanline runs entirely in the browser with no login. Details are in each product's privacy policy.

03

Kept in shape for the long run

Shipping is not the end. Rather than piling on features, the shape that already works is left alone and the details are fixed, so these stay usable for years.
